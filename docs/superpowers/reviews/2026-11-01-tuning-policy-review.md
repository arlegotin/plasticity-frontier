**Verdict: consistent with the blueprint as an explicitly declared numerical tuning convention, subject to the safeguards and limitations below.** Blueprint `docs/blueprint.md:640` requires refinement until validation uncertainty exceeds adjacent-candidate differences but does not uniquely prescribe the uncertainty statistic. Line 642 requires paired comparisons, boundary checks, objective resolution and equal treatment of families. Keeping paired rankings/diagnostics while separately defining an objective-level tolerance satisfies that distinction. It is not the uniquely mandated interpretation, and it changes the selection procedure; it must not be described as a behavior-preserving optimization or a statistical guarantee.

For independent validation tasks with per-task candidate query-MSE losses `L_a,j`, use `SE_a = sample_sd(L_a,j)/sqrt(V)` and `tau(a,b) = min(SE_a, SE_b)`. After the declared inward/interior probes and boundary checks, compare the absolute adjacent mean-loss gap with this tolerance. Task means, rather than individual query rows or repeated candidate evaluations, remain the uncertainty units. Continue selecting candidates by their recorded validation means on the same tasks; the tolerance does not redefine which candidate wins.

The diagnosis gives a sound reason to separate objective resolution from paired slope detectability: for the supplied smooth endpoint example, both the paired gap and paired SE shrink linearly with spacing, so their ratio remains above one despite diminishing absolute objective gaps. This does not establish that the distinct B04 plateau/transition trigger will resolve, that every task-loss curve is smooth, or that the proposed rule guarantees termination.

## Load-bearing limitations and safeguards

1. **Marginal objective uncertainty is not paired ranking uncertainty.** Taking the smaller marginal SE is stricter than taking the larger marginal SE; it is not generally conservative relative to paired SE. Common task difficulty/noise can make both marginal SEs large while the paired difference is measured precisely. The rule can therefore declare finite objective resolution despite statistically distinguishable adjacent candidates. Preserve the paired gap/SE and avoid labels such as “indistinguishable candidates” or “no remaining detectable improvement.”

2. **Stopping depends on the objective-level variance convention.** Adding the same task-varying, candidate-independent component to all candidate losses leaves rankings, paired differences and paired SE unchanged, yet can change the marginal SEs and stopping decision. This is an intrinsic consequence of the chosen convention, particularly relevant to common query noise. It should be stated explicitly, not hidden behind “conservative minimum SE.” Keep the loss definition fixed and do not add/subtract a common component to obtain resolution.

3. **This is finite sampled/local resolution, not an optimization or confidence certificate.** Adjacent checks near sampled minima do not exclude an unsampled interior valley, a remote better ridge value, or a discontinuity/branch transition. Mandatory inward probes must be operationally specified and recorded, including how an initial grid satisfies them; endpoint selection alone never exempts a group. The Task 10 hidden-interior B02 regression at `tests/test_selection.py:226` must still sample the better `.125` candidate and remain unresolved when a positive gap has zero uncertainty. The related endpoint regression at `:209` remains meaningful.

4. **Adaptive reuse is not new statistical evidence.** The same validation tasks drive ranking, refinement and stopping. Their estimated SEs are a descriptive precision scale, not simultaneous confidence bounds over searched candidates or an uncertainty guarantee for the adaptively selected winner. Small-V estimates may be unstable; finite observed SEs do not establish population moment assumptions. Independent preparations and the frozen protected evaluation still supply subsequent scientific inference. Do not treat repeated fits/refinements as additional validation tasks.

5. **Fairness and boundary conditions remain separate requirements.** Apply the declared tolerance to all retained family/base/tuning/rank groups and both valid neighboring sides, including ridge refinement, rather than only the ultimately winning family. Preserve zero/pseudoinverse and zero-estimator limits; extend a best finite ridge boundary as required, even if a local gap meets the tolerance. Retain all losing/failed attempts and their costs. Equal treatment means a common rule and permitted improvements, not an equal arbitrary trial count.

6. **Unresolved cases must stay unresolved.** Fewer than two independent tasks, missing/nonfinite uncertainty, or a positive gap with zero tolerance cannot authorize resolution. Exhausting the finite refinement cap, a boundary-extension limit, or representable mesh progress cannot turn an unmet check into success. A zero sampled gap with valid zero uncertainty may satisfy that local comparison only after the required probes; it still makes no unsampled-optimum claim. Resolution alone does not erase failed candidates or satisfy the other pilot/freeze gates.
