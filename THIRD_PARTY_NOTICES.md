their notices retain their own terms. Native dependencies are installed
separately, not bundled in the source package or wheel.

and wheel identities in [requirements.lock.txt](requirements.lock.txt),
[wheel_manifest.json](wheel_manifest.json) and
[the license manifest](licenses/manifest.json). Font notices are retained too.

[licenses/plotting-wheel-verification.json](licenses/plotting-wheel-verification.json).

credits the work on tangent kernels, zero-output initialization, learned gradient
geometry, linear-network factorization and statistical estimators that informed
this investigation. Citations do not grant rights in those publications.

weights were used in the released Gaussian experiment.
